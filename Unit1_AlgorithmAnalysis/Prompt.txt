Draw a recursion tree for Merge Sort dividing an array of 8 elements: [38, 27, 43, 3, 9, 82, 10, 19].
1. Show 4 distinct levels (Level 0 to Level 3):
   - Level 0: [38, 27, 43, 3, 9, 82, 10, 19]
   - Level 1: [38, 27, 43, 3] and [9, 82, 10, 19]
   - Level 2: [38, 27], [43, 3], [9, 82], [10, 19]
   - Level 3: [38], [27], [43], [3], [9], [82], [10], [19]
2. Show downward arrows for recursive division (Divide).
3. Show upward arrows for recombination in sorted order (Merge):
   - [27, 38], [3, 43], [9, 82], [10, 19]
   - [3, 27, 38, 43], [9, 10, 19, 82]
   - [3, 9, 10, 19, 27, 38, 43, 82]
4. Indicate O(N) work per level resulting in O(N log N) total time complexity.
